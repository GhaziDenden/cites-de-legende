Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Bobine is a private, single-user tool. Its only user and only data subject is the creator of the Cités de Légende channel, connecting their own accounts to publish their own content. Bobine does not have end users, a sign-up flow, or any accounts other than the operator's own.

What Bobine accesses on TikTok

Bobine uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API strictly to let the channel's operator connect and publish to their own TikTok account:

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.publish and video.upload — to upload finished, already-produced video files from the operator's own device directly to that same account (Direct Post, by file upload — never by referencing an externally hosted URL).

No other TikTok account, and no TikTok user other than the operator, is ever accessed.

What is stored, and where

Bobine runs entirely on the operator's own computer. Any access token TikTok issues is stored locally on that machine to avoid re-authenticating on every use, and is never transmitted anywhere other than to TikTok's own API. Bobine does not operate a server, a database, or any storage outside the operator's own device.

What is never done

Bobine does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. It does not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond publishing the operator's own content and confirming it was published. It does not collect data about TikTok viewers, commenters, or any account other than the operator's own.

Other platforms

The same principle applies to Bobine's existing YouTube and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) integrations: each is used solely to publish the operator's own content to the operator's own accounts.
